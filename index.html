    What the simulator is calculating

    Least-squares fit: SpectraLab fits A = mc + b, where absorbance is the y-variable and concentration is the x-variable.

    Unknown concentration: The unknown is calculated from c = (A − b) / m using the fitted slope and intercept.

    R²: The coefficient of determination indicates how closely the standards follow a straight-line calibration model.

    Built as an educational analytical chemistry simulator. Not intended for regulated laboratory reporting.
